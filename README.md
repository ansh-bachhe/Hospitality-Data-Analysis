# Hospitality Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview
The hospitality industry generates large volumes of booking and revenue data.  
Analyzing this data helps businesses understand performance, customer behavior, and revenue trends.

This project focuses on analyzing hotel booking data to derive meaningful business insights using **Excel, MySQL, and Tableau**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze hotel performance and revenue trends  
- Measure key hospitality KPIs  
- Identify peak and low demand periods  
- Understand customer booking behavior  
- Provide actionable business insights for decision-making  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, analysis & Excel dashboard  
- **MySQL** – Writing queries and KPI calculations  
- **Tableau** – Interactive dashboard and visual analysis  

---

## 📊 Key KPIs Analyzed
- **Total Bookings:** 134,590  
- **Total Revenue:** ₹1,70,87,71,229 (~₹1.71 Billion)  
- **Occupancy Rate:** 58%  
- **Cancellation Rate:** 25%  
- **Average Daily Rate (ADR):** ₹12,696  

---

## 🔍 Business Insights

### Strengths
- Strong revenue performance from **Elite & Premium** categories (≈60%)  
- High weekday revenue contribution  
- Excellent performance by **Atliq Exotica**  
- Strong markets: **Mumbai & Bangalore**
- High revenue from third-party platform bookings

### Weaknesses
- Low occupancy rate (42% rooms unused)  
- High cancellation rate (1 in 4 bookings cancelled)  
- Underperforming hotels: **Delhi & atliq Seasons**  
- No-show rate (5%) blocking room inventory  

### Opportunities
- Apply **dynamic pricing** during weak demand periods  
- Reduce cancellations with improved policies  
- Target corporate clients for weekday demand  
- Promote Standard & RT3 room categories
